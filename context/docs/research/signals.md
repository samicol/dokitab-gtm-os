## Section A: Why a facility needs DokiTab right now (buyer triggers)

### A1. HMO onboarding and insurance mandate deadlines
- NHIA Act 2022 makes health insurance compulsory; Lagos required all private HMOs to enroll clients on ILERA EKO (July 2023); a Lagos executive order makes enrollment mandatory. Sources: https://www.icirnigeria.org/?p=148001, https://nairametrics.com/2024/12/06/lagos-state-enrolls-over-1-3-million-residents-in-ilera-eko-health-scheme-in-9-years (checked 2026-11-20).
- [INFERRED] Signal: a facility newly onboarding to HMO/insurance billing (or getting hit with claim rejections) needs structured EMR + claims software. DokiTab's single testimonial targets exactly this pain ("quarterly HMO claim rejections from 18% to under 1.5%").

### A2. Accreditation and regulatory inspections
- HEFAMAA (Lagos) inspects before registration, fines unregistered facilities ₦100,000, and moved to online registration with QR verification (Dec 2025); actively seals non-compliant facilities. Sources: https://support.hefamaaportal.com.ng/kb/faq.php?id=6, https://guardian.ng/news/lagos-health-agency-steps-up-regulatory-oversight-of-hospitals/, https://nairametrics.com/2022/11/02/lagos-seals-42-health-facilities-across-the-state/ (checked 2026-11-20).
- PCN inspects pharmaceutical premises before licensing and requires sales/dispensing and patient-profile records; launched the National Electronic Pharmacy Policy (23 Dec 2025); actively seals premises. Sources: https://pcn.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Guidelines-for-Inspection-of-Pharmaceutical-Premises-January-2025.pdf, https://nairametrics.com/2025/08/22/pharmacy-council-of-nigeria-seals-486-pharmaceutical-premises-in-niger-state-over-regulatory-violations/ (checked 2026-11-20).
- [INFERRED] Signal: an upcoming HEFAMAA/PCN inspection or renewal, or a facility recently sanctioned, is a trigger to adopt record-keeping software. DokiTab markets alignment to HEFAMAA-style oversight, NDPA, and PCN 2026.

### A3. Data protection registration
- Secondary/tertiary hospitals must register as major data controllers under NDPA 2023 and secure sensitive health data. Source: https://www.mondaq.com/nigeria/data-protection/1514720/ (checked 2026-11-20).
- [INFERRED] Signal: NDPA registration or a data-protection audit creates urgency for encryption, audit trails, and in-country residency, all DokiTab first-party claims.

### A4. New branch openings / expansion
- [INFERRED] Signal: a facility opening a new branch or adding a lab/pharmacy needs multi-branch software. DokiTab markets multi-branch governance (paid plan) and standalone plug-and-play modes. No specific third-party "new branch" dataset gathered this pass; this is a category inference, not a sourced event.

### A5. Hiring roles that imply digitization
- [INFERRED] Signal: a facility hiring a health records officer, billing/claims officer, IT/EMR administrator, or lab scientist is likely digitizing or scaling operations. No specific job-board evidence pulled this pass. Follow-up: scrape Nigerian job boards (e.g. Jobberman, LinkedIn) for these roles at target facilities.

### A6. Power and internet pain
- DokiTab's entire wedge is operating through "NEPA" power cuts and internet blackouts (first-party, dokitab.com).
- [INFERRED] Signal: any facility in an area with unreliable grid/internet that has lost data or downtime on a cloud-only system is a prospect. Chronic outages are general context, not a facility-specific trigger.
