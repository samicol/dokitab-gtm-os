# DokiTab: Market, Regulatory, and Infrastructure Context

Raw research, not conclusions. All facts checked 2026-11-20. All sources below are third-party unless marked first-party.

## Data protection: Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023
- Signed into law by President Tinubu in June 2023; created the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. Source: https://dataprotection.africa/?p=20995 (checked 2026-11-20).
- Health-status information is classed as sensitive personal data. Source: https://www.mondaq.com/nigeria/data-protection/1514720/the-relevance-of-the-nigeria-data-protection-act-2023-to-healthcare-facilities-in-nigeria (checked 2026-11-20).
- Major data controllers must register with the Commission. Hospitals providing tertiary and secondary care are classified as MDP-EHL; primary health centres are classified as MDP-OHL. Same Mondaq source (checked 2026-11-20).
- Data subjects (patients) can lodge complaints with the Commission under Section 46. Same source (checked 2026-11-20).
- Conflict in sources: one says the Act replaces the NDPR 2019; another says the NDPR remains in force where it does not conflict. Sources: https://kpmg.com/ng/en/home/insights/2023/08/nigeria-data-protection-act-2023-review.html, https://fpf.org/blog/nigerias-new-data-protection-act-explained/ (checked 2026-11-20).
- NHIA health-data guidelines noted as a sector-specific rule that only partly covers the area. Source: https://www.mondaq.com/nigeria/data-protection/1514720/ (checked 2026-11-20).
- [INFERRED] Buyer urgency: facilities holding patient records must register as controllers and secure sensitive data, which favors a vendor that markets NDPA 2023 alignment, in-country data residency, encryption, and audit trails (all DokiTab first-party claims, see company.md).

## Mandatory health insurance: NHIA Act 2022 and state schemes
- NHIA Act 2022 repealed the old NHIS law and made health insurance compulsory for all Nigerians; authorizes states to establish State Health Insurance and Contributory Schemes. Source: https://www.icirnigeria.org/?p=148001 (checked 2026-11-20).
- NHIA is the national regulator; state schemes are the operational vehicle for most residents. Source: https://oxfordbusinessgroup.com/reports/nigeria/2024-report/health-education/ (checked 2026-11-20).
- Lagos (Ilera Eko / LASHMA): state scheme passed into law May 2015, administered by the Lagos State Health Management Agency (LASHMA), consumer name ILERA EKO. Source: https://nairacompare.ng/blogs/state-health-insurance-schemes-in-nigeria-explained-lagos-oyo-anambra-fct-and-more (checked 2026-11-20).
- July 2023: Lagos required all private HMOs to enroll their clients on ILERA EKO. Source: https://nairametrics.com/2024/12/06/lagos-state-enrolls-over-1-3-million-residents-in-ilera-eko-health-scheme-in-9-years (checked 2026-11-20).
- A Lagos executive order made enrollment required for residents and workers, backed by the NHIA Act 2022. Source: https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmg/ng/pdf/2024/08/Lagos State Government Issues Executive Order for the Subscription to Health Insurance.pdf (checked 2026-11-20).

## Facility regulation and accreditation
- HEFAMAA (Lagos): registers, monitors, and accredits facilities under the 2006 Lagos State Health Sector Reform Law. New facilities undergo physical inspection before a Provisional Certificate of Registration is issued. Failure to register attracts a ₦100,000 fine when discovered. Dec 2025: transitioned to fully online registration/accreditation with QR-code public verification. Sources: https://politicaleconomistng.com/?p=81403, https://support.hefamaaportal.com.ng/kb/faq.php?id=6, https://guardian.ng/news/lagos-health-agency-steps-up-regulatory-oversight-of-hospitals/ (checked 2026-11-20).
- Enforcement is active: Lagos sealed 42 facilities over irregularities/quackery. Source: https://nairametrics.com/2022/11/02/lagos-seals-42-health-facilities-across-the-state/ (checked 2026-11-20).
- PCN (Pharmacy Council of Nigeria): premises must undergo inspection before registration/licensing (Guidelines for Inspection of Pharmaceutical Premises, Jan 2025, v02); record-keeping duties include purchases/sales records and patient medication profiles. Source: https://pcn.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Guidelines-for-Inspection-of-Pharmaceutical-Premises-January-2025.pdf (checked 2026-11-20).
- National Electronic Pharmacy Policy (NEPP) launched 23 Dec 2025. Source: https://pcn.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/From-the-Registrars-Desk-January-2026.pdf (checked 2026-11-20).
- "Electronic Pharmacy Regulations 2026" reported as the primary legal framework for digital pharmaceutical operations, but only in one outlet (IT EdgeNews), not confirmed in a PCN source. Source: https://www.itedgenews.africa/nigeria-launches-electronic-pharmacy-regulations-2026-... (checked 2026-11-20). Treat as unverified.
- PCN enforcement active: sealed 486 premises in Niger State (Aug 2025). Source: https://nairametrics.com/2025/08/22/pharmacy-council-of-nigeria-seals-486-pharmaceutical-premises-in-niger-state-over-regulatory-violations/ (checked 2026-11-20).
- [INFERRED] DokiTab references "PCN 2026" and a controlled-drugs register in its pharmacy module (first-party), aligning its messaging to this tightening regime.
