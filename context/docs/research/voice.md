## Tone
- [INFERRED] Aggressive, superlative, price-led, and locally grounded. Heavy use of "#1" and "100% Free"; direct naming of competitors; local references ("NEPA", naira pricing, Nigerian HMOs and regulators). Confident and combative rather than understated.

## How it contrasts itself with legacy vendors (first-party)
- Names competitors directly: Helium Health, eClinicPlus, Hyella, Bahmni, OpenMRS, OpenEMR, Odoo, Punch Me 360, Kena, Prokip, VirtualRx.
- Price contrast, verbatim: legacy systems charge "₦25,000 to ₦50,000 per user seat" and need "₦12M–₦22M" in server rooms.
- Against paper: contrasts itself with "losing records to paper folders".
- Against tiered rivals: [paraphrase] legacy EMRs lock wards, theatre, and accounting behind higher tiers, whereas DokiTab puts them in the free tier.
