# DokiTab: Competitors

Raw research, not conclusions. All facts checked 2026-11-20. First-party = dokitab.com. Third-party = press, directories, forums.

## Competitors DokiTab names itself (first-party, dokitab.com homepage)
DokiTab's homepage explicitly names these as competitors / legacy systems it contrasts against: Helium Health, eClinicPlus, Hyella, Bahmni, OpenMRS, OpenEMR, Odoo, Punch Me 360, Kena, Prokip, VirtualRx.
- Contrast claims, verbatim where quoted: legacy systems charge "₦25,000 to ₦50,000 per user seat" and need "₦12M–₦22M" in server rooms; DokiTab contrasts itself with "losing records to paper folders" and with legacy EMRs that lock wards, theatre, and accounting behind higher tiers.
- [INFERRED] DokiTab's core wedge against all of these is price (free core + no per-seat fees + no server CapEx) plus offline-first operation during power/internet outages.

## Helium Health (third-party)
- Strongest-funded and most-cited Nigerian EMR/HMIS incumbent. Self-describes its EMR/HMIS as leading hospital software in West Africa, built to work offline and easy to deploy. Source: https://solve.mit.edu/solutions/28661 (checked 2026-11-20).
- Footprint: reported to operate in Nigeria, Ghana, Liberia, Senegal, and expanded into Saudi Arabia in 2021. Sources: https://thecondia.com/healthtech-helium-health-ng/, https://techeconomy.ng/helium-health-vs-reliance-hmo-nigeria-healthtech/ (checked 2026-11-20).
- Founders: Adegoke Olubusi (CEO), Dimeji Sofowora, Tito Ovia. Source: https://www.cnbc.com/2019/11/12/helium-health-cnbc-upstart-100.html (checked 2026-11-20).
- Sells adjacent products on top of EMR: collateral-free loans, HeliumPay, telemedicine. Source: https://techeconomy.ng/helium-health-vs-reliance-hmo-nigeria-healthtech/ (checked 2026-11-20).
- Positioning vs DokiTab: [INFERRED] Helium is the premium, well-funded, multi-country B2B incumbent; DokiTab attacks on free pricing and offline resilience.

## Other Nigeria-relevant vendors (third-party)
- SwiftPractice EMR: Lagos-based EMR vendor; CB Insights lists Helium Health as a competitor. Source: https://www.cbinsights.com/company/swiftpractice-emr (checked 2026-11-20).
- Curacel: described on a TechCabal forum as a web and mobile EMR and practice-management system for clinics; has since pivoted toward insurance infrastructure [INFERRED from general knowledge, not confirmed in this search]. Source: https://radar.techcabal.com/t/who-has-an-electronic-medical-health-record-software-solution-emr-in-nigeria/7908 (checked 2026-11-20).
- Talamus: Nigerian-origin EHR, launched 2018, noted as having made significant strides. Source: https://digitalhealthnigeria.substack.com/p/the-state-of-the-nigerian-emr-market (checked 2026-11-20).
- Easy Clinic: listed by SourceForge as AI-enabled EMR and clinic-management software; Nigerian operation not confirmed. Source: https://www.sourceforge.net/software/compare/ChartSynergy-vs-Easy-Clinic (checked 2026-11-20).
- ChartSynergy: appears as an Easy Clinic comparison on SourceForge (same source, checked 2026-11-20). Details not gathered.

## Open-source options (third-party)
- OpenMRS: open-source, "used by thousands of health facilities across Africa and globally." Source: https://digitalhealthnigeria.substack.com/p/the-state-of-the-nigerian-emr-market (checked 2026-11-20).
- Bahmni: open-source integrated hospital management system built on OpenMRS. Same source (checked 2026-11-20).
- OpenEMR: named by DokiTab as a competitor (first-party, above); open-source EMR. [INFERRED] general category knowledge.
- Positioning vs DokiTab: [INFERRED] open-source tools are free to license but carry implementation, hosting, and maintenance cost; DokiTab's pitch is free AND fully hosted/supported with offline sync, removing the self-hosting burden.

## Market dynamics (third-party)
- Analysts note vendors may favor bigger customers (insurers/HMOs) over small clinics, and that paper-to-digital transitions need substantial training investment. Sources: https://digitalhealthnigeria.substack.com/p/the-state-of-the-nigerian-emr-market, https://allbusiness.africa/insights/african-hospital-information-systems-emr (checked 2026-11-20).
- No published per-facility price points for Helium Health or any competitor were found. Pricing comparison remains a data gap.
