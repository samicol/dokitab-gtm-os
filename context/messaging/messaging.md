# DokiTab Messaging

Grounded only in `context/docs/research/` (chiefly `voice.md` and `company.md`). Persona names from `personas.md` [personas.md]. No em dashes.

## Core Message

Run your whole facility on one offline-first record for free, and keep charting and HMO claims working through every power cut [company.md], [voice.md].

## Message Pillars with Proof Points

1. **Free, with no per-seat or server cost.** Proof: "100% Free (₦0/month, zero hidden charges)", "no expiry and no per-seat fees", against legacy "₦25,000 to ₦50,000 per user seat" and ₦12M to ₦22M server rooms [company.md], [voice.md].
2. **Offline-first through outages.** Proof: during "NEPA" blackouts and internet drops, vitals, MAR doses, and SOAP notes are held locally and replayed on reconnect via an AES-256 encrypted SQLite queue [company.md], [voice.md].
3. **Fewer HMO claim rejections.** Proof: a built-in HMO claims module (NHIA, LASHMA, Hygeia, AXA) and a CMD who reports cutting rejections from 18% to under 1.5% [company.md], [customers.md].
4. **Compliant and secure by design.** Proof: NDPA 2023, NHIA Act 2022, and PCN 2026 alignment, in-country data on AWS Lagos, an immutable audit log, and biometric and geofencing controls [company.md], [market.md].

## Angle for Each Persona

- **Economic Buyer (board / owner):** run the whole hospital free, with no per-seat fees and no server room, and keep capital for clinical kit [voice.md], [company.md].
- **Champion (CMD / consultant):** one offline-first record that keeps charting through NEPA cuts and keeps HMO claims clean from the first encounter [company.md], [customers.md], [voice.md].
- **Influencer (pharmacist, lab scientist, billing/claims officer, nurse, cashier):** role-specific wins (FEFO and expiry alerts, lab panic-value alerts and HL7 feeds, clean claim submission, biometric-locked BYOD charting) [company.md].

## Words to Use

- "100% Free", "₦0/month", "no per-seat fees", "no server room" [company.md], [voice.md].
- "offline-first", "NEPA" outages, "replayed on reconnect" [company.md], [voice.md].
- "HMO claims", "claim rejections", named HMOs (NHIA, LASHMA, Hygeia, AXA) [company.md].
- Clinical terms the audience uses: SOAP, MAR, FEFO, HL7, PACS, FEFO, partograph, odontogram [voice.md].
- "Designed to satisfy HIPAA (US) security rules" (homepage phrasing) [voice.md].

## Words to Avoid

- Em dashes anywhere (house rule) [voice.md] note.
- "meets HIPAA security standards" as an unqualified claim; it is stronger than the homepage and not independently verified, so prefer the homepage phrasing [voice.md].
- Invented numbers, bed counts, customer names, or funding figures (none are verified) [customers.md], [company.md].
- "#1" and "#1 Across All Boards" presented as fact; these are DokiTab self-claims with no third-party source [company.md], [voice.md].
