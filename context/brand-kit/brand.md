# DokiTab Brand Voice

Extracted from `context/docs/research/voice.md` and `company.md`. No em dashes.

## Tone of Voice

- Aggressive, superlative, and price-led; confident and combative rather than understated [voice.md] (marked `[INFERRED]` in voice.md).
- Locally grounded: naira pricing, "NEPA" references, Nigerian HMOs, and Nigerian regulators [voice.md].
- Clinician-facing: heavy use of specific clinical and regulatory terminology, signaling domain fluency as a trust lever [voice.md].
- Directly comparative: names competitors and contrasts against paper folders and tiered legacy EMRs [voice.md].

## Compliance Phrasing Note

The homepage says "Designed to satisfy HIPAA (US) security rules" while the FAQ says the stronger "meets HIPAA security standards." Prefer the homepage phrasing; the FAQ claim is not independently verified [voice.md].
