## One-Paragraph Positioning Statement

For Nigerian and West African private hospitals, diagnostic labs, pharmacies, and specialty clinics (50 to 500+ beds) [company.md] that lose records to paper and revenue to HMO claim rejections [voice.md], [customers.md], DokiTab is an offline-first healthcare operating system, HMS, and EMR [company.md] that runs the whole facility (wards, theatre, pharmacy, lab, accounting, and HMO claims) on a free core tier with no per-seat fees and no server-room CapEx [company.md], [voice.md], and keeps charting and billing working through power and internet outages via an encrypted offline queue that replays on reconnect [company.md]. Unlike per-seat legacy EMRs that charge "₦25,000 to ₦50,000 per user seat" and need ₦12M to ₦22M server rooms [voice.md], or open-source systems that must be self-hosted [competitors.md], DokiTab is free, fully hosted, and keeps data in-country on AWS Lagos [company.md]. The one public proof point is a CMD of a multi-specialty hospital and diagnostic center in Ikeja, Lagos, who reports cutting quarterly HMO claim rejections from 18% to under 1.5% [customers.md].
